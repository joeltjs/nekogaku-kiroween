# Product: NekoGaku, Kiroween edition

NekoGaku is a Japanese study app with a haunted cat house on its home screen. It is the lite,
hackathon-sized build of a larger NekoGaku project. Category: **Frankenstein**.

## What the learner gets

- **The house** (`/`): one room only, a Halloween living room. No doors, no other rooms, no shop.
  Three cats live there: **Cecil**, **Kuku** and **Bolu**. You can pet them, pick them up, throw a
  ball, wave a feather toy, place the 10 Halloween items, take photos, and send one to the barbell
  (gym). Cats look like themselves; giving them a costume item turns them into a vampire, a ghost,
  a werewolf, a flying bat or a flying witch for a while.
- **The grimoire** (`/study`): JLPT N5, N4, N3, N2, N1 plus two SSW tracks (Kaigo nursing care and
  Food manufacturing). Every lesson is study cards with VOICEVOX audio, then a quiz.
- **The bridge**: quiz results become EXP for the cat you study with. EXP grows the cat (level 1
  kitten to level 15 senior). Petting and playing grow Love. Every answer is explained; each track
  has a mock exam with a full review.
- **Login** (`/login`): sign in with an access code issued separately (NEKO-XXXX-XXXX).

## Hard rules

1. **English everywhere** the learner reads (game and lessons). Japanese only as the thing being learned.
2. **Two cat stats only: EXP (with its level) and Love.** No hunger, energy, hygiene, coins or weather.
3. **Arcade time.** The clock does not follow real time: one in-game day lasts 10 real minutes.
4. **One season, one event: Halloween**, always.
5. **Runs on its own.** No external services, no API keys, no chat tutor.
6. **Only original or openly licensed assets.** Nothing taken from other games,
   no Harvest Moon or Dragon Quest music, no copyrighted photos. Cats, items and the room are
   drawn in code; music and sound effects are synthesized; voices come from VOICEVOX with credit.
7. The scary look is for the study UI too: dark, candle-lit, eerie, but readable (WCAG AA contrast).
